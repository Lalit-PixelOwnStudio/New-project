# Interior Designers ke liye Call Script

**Caller:** Prachi, PixelOwn Studio

**Goal:** Website bechna nahi, bas unse ek chhoti discussion fix karna aur website ke baare mein batana.

---

## Step 1: Permission lo

> "Hello, kya meri baat **[Naam]** ji se ho rahi hai?"
>
> "Kya aapke paas **2 minute** hain baat karne ke liye?"

**Agar busy hain:**

> "Koi baat nahi. Aap bata dijiye kab call karun?"

---

## Step 2: Apne baare mein batao

> "Thank you! Mera naam **Prachi** hai, main **PixelOwn Studio** se baat kar rahi hoon. Hum interior designers ke liye websites banate hain."

---

## Step 3: Jab woh poochhein "Kis regarding hai?"

Seedha pitch mat karo, ek sawaal poochho:

> "Bas ek chhota sa sawaal tha. Aapke paas abhi apna kaam dikhane ke liye koi website hai?"

---

## Step 4: Soft pitch

**Agar website nahi hai:**

> "Achha. Aapka kaam itna accha hai, ek website pe aapke saare projects ek jagah sundar tareeke se dikhenge. Client ko link bhejo, aur unhe sab kuch ek baar mein dikh jaata hai."

**Agar website hai:**

> "Bahut accha! Kya usse aapko inquiries aati hain? Agar chahein toh hum ek baar dekh ke bata sakte hain ki usme kya better ho sakta hai."

---

## Step 5: Discussion

Agar woh thoda bhi interest dikhayein ("hmm", "haan", "achha", "batao"), toh yeh 3 parts follow karo:

### 5a. Batao ki website se unhe kya fayda hoga

> "Main simple mein batati hoon. Aajkal jab kisi ko ghar ya office ka interior karwana hota hai, toh woh pehle **Google pe search** karta hai, jaise _'interior designer near me'_."
>
> "Agar aapki website hogi, toh woh log **seedha aapka kaam dekh payenge**: aapke projects, photos aur aapka style. Unhe accha laga toh woh wahin se **WhatsApp ya call karke aapko hire kar sakte hain**."
>
> "Matlab sirf referrals pe depend nahi rehna padega. **Naye clients online se bhi aayenge**, aur isse aapki **earning bhi badh sakti hai**."
>
> "Aur jab client ko pata chalta hai ki aapki proper website hai, toh woh aap pe zyada trust karta hai, aur aap apne kaam ki **sahi value** le paate ho."

### 5b. Demo link bhejne ki permission lo

> "Agar aapko thoda sa bhi interesting laga ho, toh kya main aapko **hamari demo website ka link** WhatsApp pe bhej sakti hoon? Aap free time mein aaram se dekh lena."

_(Haan bolein toh:)_

> "Thank you! Kya yeh number hi WhatsApp pe hai? Main abhi bhej deti hoon."

### 5c. Google Meet ka time fix karo

> "Aap demo dekh lena. Agar aapko theek lagega, toh hum **10-15 minute Google Meet** pe baat kar lenge. Main aapko poora samjha dungi ki aapki website kaisi banegi, aur aapke koi sawaal ho toh woh bhi clear ho jayenge."
>
> "Aapke liye kaunsa din aur time better rahega?"

_(Time note karo aur bolo:)_

> "Perfect! Main Google Meet ka link bhi WhatsApp pe bhej dungi."

**Agar woh bolein "pehle demo dekh leta hoon, phir batata hoon":**

> "Bilkul, koi jaldi nahi hai. Main 2 din baad ek baar message kar loongi, theek hai?"

---

## Step 6: Thank you bolo

> "Thank you **[Naam]** ji, aapne time diya. Have a great day!"

---

## Services Script (agar woh poochhein "Aap kya kya karte ho?")

> "Hum interior designers ke liye yeh karte hain:"

1. **Portfolio website:** Aapke projects ki photos aur gallery
2. **WhatsApp aur contact button:** Client seedha aapse baat kar sake
3. **Google pe dikhna:** Log search karein toh aapka naam aaye
4. **Updates:** Naya project ho toh hum website pe daal denge

> "Inmein se aapke liye kya sabse useful lagega?"

_(Apni actual services ke hisaab se yeh list edit kar lena.)_

---

## Agar woh mana karein

> "Bilkul samajh sakti hoon. Kya main apna number WhatsApp kar doon? Kabhi zarurat ho toh yaad kar lena. Thank you!"

---

**Yaad rakho:** Smile karke bolo, unhe zyada bolne do, aur kabhi force mat karo.
